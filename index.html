👋 How It Works

Gesture Synth uses browser-based MediaPipe AI vision to track 21 points on each hand. The left hand selects the harmony (chord and scale degree), while the right hand acts as a digital bow or wand controlling volume, inversions, and filter tone.

✋ Left Hand: Harmony (Chords)

Scale Degrees (Finger Counts):

• 1 Finger = Degree I (Tonic / Home Chord)

• 2 Fingers = Degree II

• 3 Fingers = Degree III

• 4 Fingers = Degree IV (Subdominant)

• 5 Fingers = Degree V (Dominant)

• 🤘 Index + Pinky = Degree VI

• 🤟 Index + Pinky + Thumb = Degree VII

Hand Tilt (Major vs Minor):

• ☀️ Tilt Inward (towards center): Major Mode

• 🌙 Tilt Outward (away): Minor Mode

Tip: Hold your hand open and flat facing the camera for clear finger tracking.

✋ Right Hand: Expression & Voicing

Hand Height & Movement:

• ⬆️ High in camera: Loud volume (Fortissimo)

• ⬇️ Low in camera: Soft volume (Pianissimo)

• Tilt In / Out: Audio filter sweep (Warm / Bright)

Fingers (Voicings):

• 1 Finger: Root Position Triad

• 2 Fingers: 1st Inversion

• 3 Fingers: 7th Chord

• 4 Fingers: Dominant / Diminished 7th

• 👍 Thumb Out: Drop -1 Octave for deep bass
